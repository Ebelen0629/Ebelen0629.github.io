About Me and EatEasy

Hi, I'm Edwin Belen, a student at Jefferson Community College and the creator of EatEasy.

I built this website for my Web Design class because I (and many people I know) struggle with the daily question: “What should I eat?”.

Whether you're stationed at a military base, working long hours, or just feeling indecisive, EatEasy makes it quick and fun to find the perfect meal.

The site features an interactive quiz that considers your mood, available time, and cravings, then recommends meals with direct recipe links.

You can also browse recipes, get a random suggestion, or play games to see who will make the decision.

My goal was to create a helpful, user-friendly tool that solves a real everyday problem — especially for busy military personnel, families, and students.

Thank you for visiting EatEasy! I hope it makes your food decisions a little easier and a lot more enjoyable.
